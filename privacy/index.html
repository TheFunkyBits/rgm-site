Privacy policy

Summary

Retro Games Mobile is provided by The Funky Bits. The Android application dev.thefunkybits.rgm has no RGM account system, application analytics, cloud synchronization, or application-operated server. Game files, installations, saves, settings, and play history are handled locally. External media providers process their own network and playback information as described below.

Catalog and external media

The supported-game catalog, recognition rules, and touch adaptations are included in the app. RGM does not download catalog or adaptation updates from this website; they change with app updates.

Game artwork and screenshots load over HTTPS directly from GOG's image service. GOG may receive your IP address, request time, requested image, and ordinary client information. Images may be cached in the app's local cache. See GOG's privacy policy.

RGM uses YouTube API Services. YouTube trailers are loaded into YouTube's web player only after you tap Play. Loading the player sends basic information to YouTube; playback may cause additional processing. YouTube and Google may process playback activity, IP address, browser or device information, cookies, and similar local storage, and may show their own advertising. The player identifies the app to YouTube. By using the YouTube player, you agree to YouTube's Terms of Service. See Google's privacy policy.

RGM does not append selected game files, installations, saves, settings, or play history to media requests. Opening a purchase link or a video in another app or browser is subject to that provider's policies. A cloud-backed folder provider can independently require network access.

Files you select

You choose a folder containing software files that you lawfully possess. Android grants the app read access to that selected location. RGM reads those files locally to identify a supported release and creates a private installation in the app's local storage. The selected and installed files are not uploaded by RGM.

The Android folder-access grant may remain until you revoke it in system settings, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Local data, retention, and deletion

Installed content, emulator configuration, local saves, settings, and play history remain on your device until an applicable in-app removal or reset action, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app. Image and web-player caches may be removed automatically or when app data is cleared. Uninstalling a game removes that game's local saves. RGM excludes its app-private data from Android backup and device transfer.

Clearing app data does not delete information retained independently by external providers.

Security

Media requests require HTTPS. The catalog and adaptations are packaged with the app, and local installations and saves use Android app-private storage. RGM does not upload game files or saves to a media provider.

Notifications

RGM may request Android notification permission so it can report the result of a local installation after you leave the app. The notification is generated on your device and contains no advertising or remote tracking.

Google Play

Google Play may process information independently when distributing the app, checking integrity, or reporting crashes according to Google's terms and privacy policy. RGM does not receive that information through an application analytics service.

This website

GitHub Pages hosts this static app introduction and privacy policy. The website contains no RGM analytics, tracking scripts, forms, iframes, or third-party media. GitHub may process ordinary website request information, including IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

RGM is not directed to children. External-provider processing is described above.

Changes

If a later release changes data handling, this policy and applicable store disclosures will be updated before that behavior is distributed.
