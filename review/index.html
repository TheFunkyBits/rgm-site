Review guide

Catalog data and the Android app have separate review and release routes. A version or source tag is not proof of delivered files or passing tests.

Content and rights

Catalog downloads contain descriptions, media, recognition rules, and declarative adaptation data. They contain no executable or native code and cannot download an emulator Core. RGM does not download or distribute third-party game binaries.

Catalog delivery

Released v8/v9 clients retain their original signed catalog resources. The successor route is not yet active; new catalogs use unsigned versioned HTTPS data, strict semantic validation, confined paths and an atomic offline cache that does not establish authenticity. See catalog delivery and limits of trust.
